Account and product support

For sign-in, profile, notification, subscription, or technical issues, email support@silarah.com.

Messaging and phone verification

Messaging opens after a mutual interest. Women read and send messages at no cost and do not need phone verification to message. Men verify an India +91 number before a paid Premium purchase starts, and need both active Premium and that verified number before sending. While Premium is active, change a verified number from Profile → Trust checks → Phone number and confirm the new OTP. Premium remains attached to the same Silarah account until its store-provided expiry date; changing the phone number does not cancel or shorten it.

Safety

Use in-app blocking and reporting first. For urgent account-safety escalation, email safety@silarah.com.

Privacy

Download an immediate machine-readable archive in Profile → Settings → Privacy. For assisted access, correction, an omitted export category, deletion, consent withdrawal or privacy questions, email privacy@silarah.com or read the Privacy Rights & Grievance Policy.

Grievances

Imran Ahmed is Silarah’s grievance officer. Email grievance@silarah.com with your account email, concise description, relevant dates and requested outcome. We acknowledge formal grievances within 24 hours and ordinarily resolve them within 7 days; qualifying urgent unlawful or intimate-content complaints follow shorter legal deadlines.
