Privacy

Download an immediate machine-readable archive in Profile → Settings → Privacy. For assisted access, correction, an omitted export category, deletion, consent withdrawal or privacy questions, email privacy@silarah.com or read the Privacy Rights & Grievance Policy.

Grievances

The Silarah Grievance Desk receives formal grievances at grievance@silarah.com. Include your account email, a concise description, relevant dates and requested outcome. We acknowledge formal grievances within 24 hours and ordinarily resolve them within 7 days; qualifying urgent unlawful or intimate-content complaints follow shorter legal deadlines.
