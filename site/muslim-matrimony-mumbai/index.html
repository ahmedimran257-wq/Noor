Knowing Mumbai

What families here
often weigh.

Every family is different. These are common considerations, not assumptions about you.

Many communities, one city

Mumbai’s Muslims include Konkani Muslims, Dawoodi Bohras, Khojas and Memons, Urdu-speaking families with roots in Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, and Marathi-speaking households. Some families prefer to marry within their community or jamaat; others are open. State what matters to you plainly, and respect what matters to others.

Careers, commutes and housing

Long working hours, local-train commutes and the cost of housing shape married life in Mumbai. Being honest about profession, work schedule and whether you expect joint or separate living saves both families time.

Across the metropolitan region

Introductions often span Thane, Navi Mumbai, Mira-Bhayandar, Bhiwandi and Kalyan. A thirty-kilometre distance can mean a long commute here, so set distance preferences realistically.

The first meeting

Choose a busy public café or restaurant near a convenient station and share your plans with family. If elders prefer to be part of the first conversation, connect a guardian in Silarah so their role is visible.

Before you share anything personal

Keep conversations in the app until trust develops. Never send money, OTP codes or identity documents, and report anyone who asks. Read the Safety Centre for more.
