Knowing Kolkata

What families here
often weigh.

Every family is different. These are common considerations, not assumptions about you.

Bengali and Urdu-speaking communities

Many Kolkata Muslims speak Bengali at home, while neighbourhoods such as Park Circus, Rajabazar and Metiabruz—where the exiled Nawab of Awadh settled in the nineteenth century—have deep Urdu and Hindi-speaking roots. Some families prefer a shared mother tongue; others do not mind. Your preference is yours to set.

Education and steady careers

Families often place weight on education, a stable profession and good character. Being clear about qualifications, work and future plans helps introductions start on solid ground.

From the city to the districts

Introductions may connect Kolkata with Howrah, Hooghly, Murshidabad, Malda and other districts, as well as with relatives in other states. Distance and location preferences let you decide how far to look.
