Trust & Verification Policy

What photo, phone, email, guardian and account-history checks establish, and how temporary photo captures are handled.

1. Types of check

Silarah may offer profile-photo review, SMS phone confirmation, email confirmation, guardian connection and established-member indicators. These are separate trust signals. They do not establish legal identity and are not a comprehensive background check.

2. Temporary photo captures

The optional photo check uses three guided captures: look at the camera, give a gentle smile and blink once. Normal glasses, hijab, niqab and culturally modest clothing are allowed. An accessibility fallback lets a member capture a step when smile or blink guidance does not work.

3. Human decision

On-device face signals provide capture guidance only. An authorized human compares the temporary captures with the current primary profile photo. An on-device score never grants or rejects the badge. Silarah does not create facial embeddings or templates, perform government-ID matching or estimate age from a face.

4. Badge meaning

A Photo verified badge means only that temporary captures reasonably matched the profile photo at the review date. A Phone verified badge means an SMS code was successfully confirmed; buying Premium alone does not grant it. Badges do not certify character, intentions, marital status, finances, criminal history or safety.

5. Security and retention

Temporary captures are stored privately and shown to authorized staff only through short-lived previews. They are deleted from active verification storage after review and no later than 48 hours after submission. Silarah retains the decision, dates, reviewer audit and deletion result without retaining the captures.

6. Changes and appeals

Photo verification is paused when the primary profile photo changes or a credible impersonation concern requires review. Members may submit again or appeal through safety@silarah.com. Include the account email and decision date, but do not email photos, passwords or OTP codes.

7. Regional availability and messaging rules

The initial service launch is limited to enabled launch markets shown in the app; during the India launch only +91 numbers can be verified. Silarah will never ask for your email or phone OTP outside the official in-app flow. Women do not need phone confirmation for free messaging. Premium is activated before phone confirmation becomes available, and men complete SMS confirmation before sending. While Premium is active, a confirmed number can be changed from Profile → Trust checks → Phone number using a new OTP. The change does not cancel or shorten an existing store subscription.
