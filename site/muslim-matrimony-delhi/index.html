Knowing Delhi

What families here
often weigh.

Every family is different. These are common considerations, not assumptions about you.

Old Delhi heritage, a newer city around it

Shahjahanabad—today’s Old Delhi around Jama Masjid—has been home to Muslim families for centuries, while areas such as Jamia Nagar, Okhla and Zakir Nagar grew more recently. Families differ in how much heritage, language or neighbourhood matter; your preferences reflect your own priorities.

Families from many states

Many Delhi households have roots in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Uttarakhand, Kashmir or Punjab, and introductions often involve relatives in those states. Mother-tongue and location preferences help you include—or focus beyond—those connections.

A region, not just a city

Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Gurugram and Faridabad can be an hour or more apart. Set realistic distance preferences, and be clear about where you expect to live after marriage.
