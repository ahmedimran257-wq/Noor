Knowing Hyderabad

What families here
often weigh.

Every family is different. These are common considerations, not assumptions about you.

A city with its own Muslim heritage

Hyderabad’s Muslim culture was shaped by centuries of Qutb Shahi and Asaf Jahi rule, and Dakhni—the Deccani form of Urdu—is still heard across homes and markets. Many families value that shared idiom and tehzeeb, while others care more about practice and outlook than dialect. Silarah treats mother tongue as one preference you choose, not a rule.

Families who think about distance

It is common for Hyderabadi families to have relatives in the Gulf, the UK or the US, and for a spouse to be asked whether they would relocate. Setting relocation and post-marriage living preferences early helps both families avoid misunderstandings later.

Two very different daily lives

The Old City and the technology corridors of HITEC City and Gachibowli can mean different routines, commutes and expectations about joint or nuclear living. Being specific about profession, work hours and living arrangements makes introductions more realistic.

The first meeting

For a first meeting, choose a busy family restaurant or a café in a well-known area and tell a family member where you will be. Many Hyderabadi families prefer that elders meet first; you can connect a guardian or wali in Silarah to make that easier.

Before you share anything personal

Keep conversations in the app until trust develops. Never send money, OTP codes or identity documents, and report anyone who asks. Read the Safety Centre for more.
