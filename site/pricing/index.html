Why we do not claim “cheapest in the world”

₹299 per month and ₹749 every 3 months are deliberately accessible, but global superlative price claims are difficult to verify across currencies, temporary offers and changing competitors. We prefer precise prices you can check in Google Play before paying.

What the new relationship tools mean

Compatibility insights explain alignment using profile information and preferences that members chose to state. They do not assess character, safety or the chance of marriage, and they do not expose another member’s raw preference values. Shortlist categories, notes and reminders remain private to their author. Incognito removes a profile from general discovery and search while Premium remains active, but people with a prior interest, match, approved photo-access or guardian relationship may still access it.

Release availability
