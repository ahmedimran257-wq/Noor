Our purpose

Marriage deserves more than a swipe.

Silarah is building a calmer Muslim matrimony experience for adults who want clarity, dignity and serious intention.

Thoughtful by design

Profiles give space to values, marriage timeline, faith, lifestyle, family and the practical preferences that shape a shared future.

Privacy as a product choice

Members can control photo visibility, pause discovery, block contact, report concerns and delete their account through understandable controls.

Family aware, member controlled

Optional guardian and wali tools make room for trusted family involvement without removing an adult member's authority over their account.

Trust without false certainty

Verification badges have specific meanings and limits. Photo verification is separate from identity review, and neither replaces judgment or guarantees another person's intentions.
