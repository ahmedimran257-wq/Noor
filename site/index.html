Good questions deserve clear answers

Before you
begin.

Need something more specific? Talk to our support team.

Is Silarah a dating app?+

No. Silarah is designed for adults seeking serious Muslim matrimonial introductions. Product choices focus on compatibility, intention and respectful pacing rather than casual engagement.

Who can join?+

Silarah is strictly for adults aged 18 or older who are legally able to marry and are seeking a serious matrimonial introduction.

Where is Silarah available?+

Silarah is launching in India first. Discovery can cover anywhere in India or prioritize the same city, state, or a selected distance. More countries can be enabled later after their billing, safety, support and legal requirements are ready.

How does verification work?+

Photo verification uses guided look, smile and blink captures followed by human comparison with the current profile photo. Captures are deleted after review and within 48 hours. It is not proof of legal identity.

Can a wali or guardian participate?+

Optional guardian tools can support family involvement where enabled. They are transparent and do not transfer ownership or control of an adult member's account.

Are my profile photos private?+

You can choose visibility for everyone, after mutual interest, or by individual request. Signed media access and app controls help enforce the selected setting.

How do I report someone?+

Use the in-app block and report controls from a profile or conversation. For urgent safety concerns, contact safety@silarah.com. Contact local emergency services when anyone is in immediate danger.

Is Silarah free?+

Core access and available premium options are shown clearly in the app. Prices and entitlements depend on platform, country and current store availability.

How do I delete my account?+

Delete from Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or use our public account-deletion page if you cannot access the app.
