Questions,
answered honestly.

Need something more specific? Talk to support.

Is Silarah a dating app?+

No. Silarah is built for adults seeking serious Muslim matrimonial introductions. Profiles and matching focus on compatibility, intention and respectful pacing.

How much does Premium cost?+

The India launch price is ₹300 per month. Google Play shows the final price, renewal date, taxes and cancellation terms before purchase.

Why do women message free?+

Women can read and send messages after a mutual match without a subscription or phone verification. Men require active Premium and an SMS-verified phone to send. This is Silarah’s current India messaging model.

Where is Silarah available?+

Silarah launches in India first. Other listed countries remain marked “Coming later” until their billing, safety, support and legal readiness is complete.

Is there an iPhone app?+

iOS is planned after the Android launch is stable and App Store preparation is complete. No public iOS release date has been promised yet.

Does Silarah collect government ID or biometrics?+

No government ID is required for the current photo check, and Silarah does not create or retain a face template. Guided captures are reviewed by a person and deleted after review and within 48 hours.

Can I control who sees my photos?+

Yes. Choose visibility for everyone, after an accepted interest, or by approving each photo request individually.

Can a guardian or wali participate?+

Optional guardian tools support transparent family involvement where enabled. They do not transfer ownership or control of an adult member’s account.

How do I block, report or delete my account?+

Block and report from a profile or conversation. Delete through Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or use the public deletion page.
