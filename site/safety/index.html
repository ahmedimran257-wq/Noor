Control your visibility

Choose whether photos are visible to everyone, after mutual interest, or by request. Pause your profile when you want to leave discovery without deleting your account. Your exact address is not displayed to other members.

Block and report

Use the profile or conversation menu to block contact and submit a report. Reports are reviewed with relevant context and may lead to warnings, content removal, visibility limits, verification, suspension or permanent bans.

Verification has limits

A photo-verified badge indicates that temporary guided captures reasonably matched the current profile photo during human review. It does not prove legal identity, character or intentions and is not a criminal, marital, financial or comprehensive background check.

Protect yourself

Keep conversations in-app while trust develops. Never send money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, bank credentials or OTP codes. Verify important claims independently, involve trusted people when appropriate and meet in a public place.

Immediate danger

Silarah is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services first. For platform safety concerns, use in-app reporting or email safety@silarah.com.
