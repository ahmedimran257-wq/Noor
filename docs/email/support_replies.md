# Silarah support replies

Ready-to-send replies for support@silarah.com. Save each one as a Gmail
template (Settings > See all settings > Advanced > Templates: Enable, then
compose the reply and choose More > Templates > Save draft as template).

House style:

- Open with "Assalamu alaikum {first name}," and thank them for writing.
- Say what you did or found, in one or two sentences, before asking anything.
- One clear next step. No "Test", one-word bodies or all-caps subjects: spam
  filters treat short, generic mail as suspicious.
- Close with "Warm regards," and let the signature carry the brand.
- Never ask for OTP codes, passwords, card or bank details.
