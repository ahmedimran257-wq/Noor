# Google Play reviewer instructions

## Access

Silarah requires authentication. Provide Google Play with a dedicated synthetic
review account that is not protected by a personal phone number, expiring inbox,
IP allowlist or one-time manual approval. Store its credentials only in Play
Console, never in this repository.

The account should have:

- onboarding completed;
- a safe synthetic profile and at least two licensed test photos;
- visible discovery status;
- one synthetic mutual interest/conversation for chat review;
- no real identity document or real member messages.

If OTP authentication cannot be bypassed safely, provide a stable review inbox
and exact steps for retrieving the code. Confirm this flow immediately before
submission.

## Navigation paths

1. **Discovery:** sign in, open Discover, use Filters to change city/country and
   enable diaspora/global discovery.
2. **Interests:** open Interests to review received and sent interest states.
3. **Chat:** open the prepared accepted connection. Typing presence and message
   translation are available inside the conversation.
4. **Photo privacy:** Profile → Manage photos → choose everyone, mutual interest,
   or request-to-view → Save photos.
5. **Verification:** Profile → Trust & identity. Verification is optional and a
   strong badge is granted only after the relevant checks/review.
6. **Safety:** open a member profile → menu → Report or Block.
7. **Account controls:** Profile → Settings → Pause profile, Help & support, or
   Delete account.
8. **Legal:** Profile → Settings → Legal & privacy.
9. **Subscription:** open Premium. Products and localized prices are supplied by
   Google Play/RevenueCat and may be unavailable outside an authorized test track.

## Reviewer notes

- Adults only (18+); there is no child-directed experience.
- User-generated photos are checked before publication and reports are available.
- Some identity verification routes depend on country. Global applicants can use
  the manual review route; no badge is granted merely for starting OAuth.
- Contact for review issues: `support@silarah.com`.
